02 / Our roots

FROM THE
BACKYARD
TO THE
BARBERSHOP.

It all started in a backyard in 2011. What began as a passion for good cuts and good company grew into a loyal community — and by 2015, Backyard Barbers had its own shop.

Today, the team brings that same laid-back energy and attention to detail to every appointment in Millner. No unnecessary fuss. Just a proper cut and a place you can feel at home.
